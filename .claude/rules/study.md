# Listening study rules

- **Audio only.** No video, no performer names, no labels such as "professional" (Tsay 2013;
  Kroger and Margulis 2017).
- Counterbalance presentation order; listeners favor the second item.
- Every stimulus in a study uses one fixed renderer and one loudness normalization, so recording
  quality is not a cue.
- Compare only within the same passage.
- Record listener background (musician / non-musician, years of training) and analyze the two
  groups separately as well as together.
- Target at least 20 comparisons per item on average (total judgements at least 10 x items), with
  scale-separation reliability of at least .8, and also report split-half reliability (Kinnear et al. 2025;
  Verhavert et al. 2019; see landscape 1.4). The earlier "12-17" figure was unsupported. If pair selection is adaptive, estimate reliability on a
  non-adaptive holdout (Bramley).
- Collect no personal data beyond what the protocol needs. Consent text and recruiting are OWNER
  (Henry) decisions. Agents draft; they do not launch.
